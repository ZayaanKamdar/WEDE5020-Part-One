## Project Overview

This project involves the planning and development of a website for Manna Bakehouse, an artisan bakery located in Gillitts, KwaZulu-Natal.

Manna Bakehouse specialises in artisan sourdough bread and speciality bread products. The proposed website is intended to provide the business with an informative online presence where customers can learn more about the bakery, view its products and find important business information.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

The main goal of the website is to create an informative and easy-to-use online presence for Manna Bakehouse.

The objectives of the website are to:

- Showcase Manna Bakehouse's artisan sourdough bread and speciality products.
- Provide information about the bakery and its background.
- Make the bakery's location and contact information easy to find.
- Attract new customers from Gillitts, Hillcrest, Kloof and surrounding areas.
- Provide simple navigation between the different website pages.
- Strengthen the identity of Manna Bakehouse as a local artisan bakery.

---

## Key Features and Functionality

The proposed website consists of five main pages:

1. **Home – index.html**  
   Introduces Manna Bakehouse and provides access to the main sections of the website.

2. **About Us – about.html**  
   Provides information about the history and background of Manna Bakehouse.

3. **Our Bread – bread.html**  
   Provides information about the artisan sourdough and speciality bread products.

4. **Gallery – gallery.html**  
   Provides a section for displaying bakery and product content.

5. **Contact Us – contact.html**  
   Provides the bakery's contact and location information.

Navigation links are included to allow users to move between the different pages of the website.
