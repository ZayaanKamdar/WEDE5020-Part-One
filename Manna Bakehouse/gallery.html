About Manna Bakehouse

A local artisan bakery with a strong focus on traditional sourdough bread.

Our Story

Manna Bakehouse developed from an artisan sourdough operation known as Super Sourdough, which originally operated from a garage in Winston Park.

After the previous owners decided to emigrate in 2020, Tim Keep acquired the business and later established Manna Bakehouse in Gillitts (The Crest Magazine, 2023).

The bakery's bread-making process focuses on traditional sourdough methods using simple ingredients such as purified water, stoneground flour and salt.

Our Artisan Process

Natural Ingredients Purified water, flour and salt form the foundation of traditional sourdough.
Slow Fermentation Sourdough develops flavour through a longer fermentation process.
Hand Crafted Artisan bread focuses on careful preparation and traditional baking techniques.

Competitor Research

The Bread Mill was used as a competitor during the research stage of this project.

The Bread Mill is a well-established family-owned bakery in Durban. It states that it was established in 1987 and sells a broad range of products including breads, pies, designer cakes and doughnuts (The Bread Mill, n.d.).

While The Bread Mill focuses on a large variety of baked products, Manna Bakehouse can differentiate itself through its stronger focus on artisan sourdough and speciality breads.
